# Jason — Autotask Parts, Stock, Purchase Orders and Ticket Charges

## Section Goal

Manage procurement evidence from ticket/parts request through stock availability, purchase authorization, Autotask PO and line-item readback, partial/full receiving, staging, technician assignment, installation, return, ticket charge and audit. Reuse the **same** production-style Teams Universal Approver implemented for #2043; do not create a second sender, money approval service or provider bypass. No user-visible shipment, charge or inventory commitment may be reported as complete without verified Autotask provider readback.

## Source of truth

- **Autotask PSA** is authoritative for ticket-company relationship, product ID, inventory location/product quantities, Autotask PO, PO line item, receiving record, serialized item, stock transfer and ticket charge.
- Jason's existing `service.inventory.item.search` is the new **read-only** capability: exact positive `product_id` plus `inventory_location_id` selectors only; no raw query, broad scan or free-text identifiers. It uses the existing Autotask provider connector, central orchestrator identity/authority and information-release checks, with no new credential. Reads use the documented `InventoryItems` REST resource, whose `quantityOnHand` must cover `quantityPicked` + `quantityReserved`; current available candidate is `on hand - picked - reserved`. The first-read max is 2 only to detect duplicate/ambiguous records. A result other than exactly one authoritative product/location or older than 10 minutes is rejected.
- If `serial_required`, InventoryItems aggregate is insufficient to prove the exact serial. Require a separate governed `InventoryItemSerialNumbers` and serial-level allocation before permitting stock usage. **This child capability is not yet implemented** and must not be inferred from catalog/quantity alone.
- The existing `ProcurementTeamsFlow.check_parts_stock` invokes `GovernedPartsAvailabilityWorkflow` with the actual signed Teams principal and existing `ProcurementTeamsFlow._read` (no direct provider API). Its response is a **stock candidate**, never a reservation. Nothing may be staged/assigned/charged on this read alone. The resulting `SQLitePartsLifecycleLedger` stores only append-only provider evidence and immutable linked ticket/company/product/location scope.

## Spending / Teams approvals

- Technician's exact Autotask Contact UDF **`Spending limit`** (companyID=0, authenticated AOT requester) is the ceiling for **unapproved** spending. A reviewed supervisor policy may only lower it. Sum line costs, freight, taxes and fees. All above-ceiling commitments must get a separately signed, current, exact-scope approval through the **same** TeamsGatewayPlaybookApprovalSender, SignedTeamsApprovalEvidenceVerifier and ApprovalRequestService as production releases. The card has the same mobile-first look, only Approve/Deny, expiry, original card nonce, immutable request and audit. This is approval of *purchase only*, not a production release/CCC approval.
- `require_po_authority` delegates above-threshold authorization to the reusable `require_signed_purchase_approval` helper from #2043, validating immutable approved request, Teams channel evidence hash, exact request ID, scope digest, actor and organization. A bare `approval_id`, supervisor role selected in a message, expired card, policy-change, forged receipt or PO total above the UDF without approval is denied.
- The existing Autotask PO/ticket charge governed execution logic must continue to perform actual provider writes and post-mutation readback. Parts lifecycle events are **evidence receipts**, not provider mutation commands. The event ledger never directly calls Autotask, restarts a service, sends a Teams approval or mutates stock.

## State machine and inventory handling

1. **Requested** — trusted company, Autotask ticket, product, inventory location, technician, quantity, destination; scope SHA-256 is immutable and idempotent.
2. **Stock checked** — fresh exact provider read with trusted correlation, quantitative evidence and normalized SHA-256; no reservation claimed. If some units on hand and some needed, split into immutable stock and PO child scopes with the exact same ticket; do not double count them.
3. **Stock reserved** — only after independent provider readback proving a strictly matching stock-reserved quantity increase for the correct product/location/ticket. Such provider reservation/transfer write capability is **not yet implemented**; until it exists, candidate stock must remain unallocated and Human Review applies. Never silently decrement a local count in place of Autotask.
4. **Purchase approved → PO created** — match source submission digest, company, requester, ticket, technician Contact UDF and signed Teams approval when over limit; require actual governed PO creation and readback. Existing multi-line PO plans/line-item uniqueness and idempotency logic remain authoritative.
5. **Partially received / received** — require matching Autotask PO receiving readbacks, positive counts, idempotent event IDs and never more than the requested quantity. `PurchaseOrderItemReceiving` integration and live partial-receipt validation remain subject to provider acceptance.
6. **Staged / assigned / installed / returned** — keep accountable technician and physical location. Reuse Jason's existing `AllocationPlan` and `ReleaseGate`; a client-assigned part requires exact ticket and confirmed ticket charge or authoritative no-charge disposition **before release**. Installation cannot exceed verified stock allocated/received. Serialized parts require distinct serial proof. A return is not completed without Autotask stock readback; a message alone is not sufficient.
7. **Ticket charged / reconciled** — exact Autotask charge ID, company and ticket readback; unique per-line charge ID, append-only evidence and event replay safeguards. Existing invoice/billing leakage assurance remains responsible for downstream invoice posting and reconciliation.

## Reusable implementation

`parts_inventory_lifecycle.py` owns the provider-neutral `PartsScope`, `InventoryEvidence`, `PartsPlan`, `SQLitePartsLifecycleLedger`, append-only idempotent event transitions, vendor/PO/receiving/stock/ticket charge evidence guards, exact monetary comparison and parent-to-child inventory splitting. `parts_availability_workflow.py` contains only the narrow orchestrator adapter for a canonical authenticated inventory read, not a new connector. `provider_read_argument_adapter.py` compiles the exact product/location query through the shared Autotask `autotask.entity.query`; `procurement_teams_flow.py` exposes `check_parts_stock` to existing procurement callers. Shared #2043 `require_signed_purchase_approval` controls the PO approval evidence; existing PO mutation and `AllocationPlan`/`ReleaseGate` implementations remain the execution authority.

## Completion / acceptance criteria

- Static tests: exact product/location read and zero broad querying; stale/ambiguous/mismatched/serialized stock fails closed; no stock reserved on read alone; idempotent ledger; cross-ticket and changed purchase scope rejected; under and over UDF thresholds; real canonical signed approval receipt required; PO and receipt provider readbacks; no duplicate charge; no staging handoff before ticket billing; returns/serial assignments audited. All must pass without live provider writes.
- **Still to implement for live acceptance:** governed Autotask stock reservation/pick/transfer provider mutation APIs; serial-level stock lookup; actual Teams parts request/receiving user interaction and notification; controlled client/ticket/provider readback and end-to-end purchase/partial-receipt/return/charge verification; owner-approved exact-SHA protected production deployment. The `InventoryItems` entity may require enabled Autotask Inventory module/API security permissions.
- Stop and notify a technician if the provider denies Inventory permission, stock is stale or ambiguous, PO/receipt cannot be matched, or the signed Teams Universal Approver has not been functionally accepted. **Do not close #2044 or declare stock automation functional based solely on code merge/tests.**
