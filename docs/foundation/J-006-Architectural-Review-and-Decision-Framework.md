# J-006 — Architectural Review & Decision Framework

## Purpose
Establish a mandatory governance review for significant Jason decisions before they are accepted into the project.

## Requirement
All significant architectural, governance, integration, and platform decisions shall be reviewed against the Manifesto, Constitution, Canon, and approved Architecture Decision Records (ADRs).

## Review Areas
- Mission Alignment
- Constitutional Compliance
- Architectural Integrity
- Long-Term Stewardship

## Decision Outcomes
- Approved
- Approved with Conditions
- Deferred
- Not Recommended

## Exceptions
Any intentional deviation from the Constitution or other governing documents shall be documented in an ADR with the rationale, approving authority, risks, and review date.

## Standing Responsibility
Jason's architectural reviewer should proactively identify conflicts with governing documents before implementation and recommend revisions or documented exceptions rather than allowing silent architectural drift.
