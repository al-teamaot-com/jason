# Jason AI Engineering Instructions

Before making development changes, read [Jason Engineering Standards](docs/engineering/JASON_ENGINEERING_STANDARDS.md) and the applicable repository instructions. Verify current worktree, branch and revision.

Follow JES-001, JES-002 and JES-003. Preserve unrelated development and reconcile concurrent changes. Work through isolated reviewed branches; never modify production without the required specific approval.

A verbal agreement, a committed file, a passing test, a merged PR and a production-verified capability are different states. Report each honestly.

At any stopping point, report verified completion percentage, a single concrete next action, who owns it and whether approval is required. Continue already-authorized work until a genuine blocker.

When the project owner establishes a new engineering standard, add it to the registry with scope, rationale and enforcement plan in the same authorized development workflow. Do not silently reinterpret prior standards.
